Quick answers

Cancel or change your subscription

muno Unlimited is billed by Apple, so the subscription is managed in your Apple Account: open iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions, or in the app go to Settings → Unlimited potential. Cancelling stops the next charge and nothing else: every word, deck and language you collected stays yours and stays practisable.

Restore purchases

New phone, a reinstall, or the app forgot you subscribed? Make sure the device is signed into the Apple Account you purchased with, then open Settings → Unlimited potential and tap Restore purchases at the bottom of the screen.

Refunds

Payments are processed by Apple, not by us — we never see your card and cannot charge or refund it ourselves. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com, signed in with the Apple Account you paid with.

Delete your account

In the app: Settings → Account → Delete account. It removes your account and everything in it — every word, deck and language — immediately and permanently. Deleting a single deck is gentler: it waits in Recently deleted for 30 days and can be restored from there.

Your data

What muno stores, who processes it and why is written down in the Privacy Policy. Any privacy request — a copy of your data, a correction, deletion — goes to the address above and is answered within a month, as the GDPR requires.
