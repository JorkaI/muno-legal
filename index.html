What happens when you add a word

This is the one part of muno worth reading closely, because it involves sending text off our own servers.

When you save a new word, muno needs to produce a translation and spoken audio for it. To do that, the word itself is sent to Google, which returns the translation and the audio. Only the word or phrase and the two languages involved are sent. Your email address, your account identifier and the rest of your collection are not sent.

Generated cards are cached in a shared dictionary so that the same word does not have to be generated twice. That cache holds words, not people: it contains no link to the account that first requested the word.

Where your data is stored

Your account and word collection are stored in our Supabase project in the Central EU (Frankfurt) region, inside the European Union.

Some of the providers above are based in the United States, so certain processing takes place outside the European Economic Area. Those transfers rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses, or on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework where the provider is certified under it.

How long we keep it

  • Your account and word collection: for as long as your account exists. You can delete your account yourself at any moment, in the app — Settings → Account → Delete account — and everything is removed at once; or ask us and we will do it.
  • Server logs: a short rolling window, never longer than 30 days, after which they are discarded.
  • The shared word cache: kept indefinitely, because it is not personal data and is not linked to any account.
  • Purchase records: Apple retains transaction records under its own terms and its own retention periods, which we do not control.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask us to:

  • tell you what data we hold about you, and give you a copy of it,
  • correct anything that is wrong,
  • delete your account and everything in it,
  • restrict or object to a particular use of your data,
  • export your data in a portable format,
  • withdraw any consent you have given, at any time.

The fastest way to delete your account is inside the app itself — Settings → Account → Delete account — which removes your account and everything in it immediately, with no email needed.

For everything else, write to jgahokidze@gmail.com and we will respond within one month, as the GDPR requires. We will not charge you and we will not ask you to justify the request.

If you are unhappy with how we handled your request, you can complain to the Polish supervisory authority, the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (Prezes Urzędu Ochrony Danych Osobowych), ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warsaw, Poland, or to the data protection authority in your own country.

Children

muno is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under that age. If you believe a child has created an account, write to us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change how muno handles data, we will update this page and change the date at the top. If a change materially affects your rights, we will tell you in the app before it takes effect.
